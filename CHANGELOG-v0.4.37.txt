v0.4.37 - Auto Game Window and Chat HUD Capture
- WGC connects to Maple HWND and captures a single full-client probe frame.
- Detects the blue Maple chat-toolbar button near the estimated bottom HUD.
- Automatically switches the WGC capture source to the OCR chat ROI; no screen drag.

- MSS fallback computes game-relative estimated ROI without asking to select a region.
- Existing manual crop is still available by disabling automatic detection in settings.
- OCR/translation models, source palette, OBS and rendering pipeline preserved.
